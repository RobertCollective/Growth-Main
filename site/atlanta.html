FAQs

Who is growth for?+

Entrepreneurs, business owners, and investors who want practical insights into business, AI, and wealth.

What should I bring?+

Bring your ticket and a photo ID. A notebook can help you capture ideas you want to put into practice.

What should I wear?+

Business casual is recommended. Bring an extra layer for cooler indoor temperatures.

Can I bring a guest?+

Yes. Every attendee, including your guest, needs a valid ticket.

How does accessible seating work?+

Accessible seating varies by venue. Contact the growth team before booking to discuss your needs and confirm availability.

Will an agenda be published?+

We do not publish a detailed agenda. Live programming may change.
